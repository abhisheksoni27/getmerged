# BRAND.md

## Mission

Write blogs that make technically dense subjects feel approachable, practical, and human. The goal is not to impress readers with jargon—it's to leave them smarter, more confident, and actually able to use what they just read.

## Topics and Scope

- Backend engineering, system design, and production tradeoffs
- Go and distributed systems
- Open source contribution and developer tooling
- Personal experiments, AI tinkering, and automation
- Data-backed analysis from original projects such as GetMerged and Opendoor

## Positioning

This is not a textbook. It is a personal engineering blog written from the perspective of someone who builds this stuff in production, breaks it, fixes it, and then explains it in plain language.

The voice is intentionally direct and informal, closer to explaining something on a whiteboard than writing academic prose. Readability and transfer matter more than sounding clever.

## Differentiation

- First-hand project context over generic tutorials
- Concrete examples and runnable code over vague theory
- Original data and measurement over copied benchmarks
- Practical tradeoffs over idealized architectures
- Conversational delivery over formal reporting

## Content Standards

- Claims should be verifiable or clearly labeled as experience
- Statistics need a source, a date, and a retrieval note when cited
- Code examples should be simplified for clarity but never fake
- Self-promotion is allowed once per post when it is genuinely relevant
- No filler, no padding, no recycled listicles without original framing

## Audience

Developers who are smart but busy. Beginners who want to understand the "why" behind the code. Operators who need to make a decision in the next ten minutes.

## Voice Anchor

Write like you are explaining it to a curious friend who is smart but does not work in your exact stack. Use the voice in VOICE.md as the source of truth for tone, pacing, and analogy style.
