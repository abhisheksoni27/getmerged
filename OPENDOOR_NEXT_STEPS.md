### 8 fresh (non‑obvious, zero‑budget) ideas with embedded virality

These aren’t the usual suspects. Each one turns OpenDoor from a “look‑up tool” into a sharable, story‑driven, everyday product.

1. **“Find My Door” public bounty thread**  
   A contributor pastes their tech stack + what they want to work on (e.g., “Rust, networking, beginner”). OpenDoor suggests 3 doors. The suggestion becomes a public, shareable card (“Help Alex find their first merge”). Bystanders vote, comment, reshare. Every bounty doubles as organic distribution.

2. **“Repo Roast & Warm Hug” share‑cards**  
   AI generates a light, brutal‑funny roast of a repo’s contribution experience (slow reply, bus factor 1) or a glowing “warm hug” (first‑timer merge in 4 hours). Maintainers share roasts to prove they’re fixing things; contributors share hugs as bragging rights. One click → image card → social feeds.

3. **“Merge Guarantee” (30‑day pledge)**  
   Promise: use Opendoor’s blueprint, pick a C‑Rank A+ repo, open a PR, and if it doesn’t get merged in 30 days, Opendoor gives you a public “Ghost Whisperer” badge (and maybe a free Pro month). The guarantee itself is a story. Success stories are viral proof; failure badges become ironic trophies people still share.

4. **Live “Welcome Leaderboard” (weekly top‑10 repos)**  
   Every Monday, publish a ranked list of the 10 most welcoming repos that week. Automatically generate a “#1 most welcoming repo” badge for the maintainer. Maintainers share their ranking proudly. The leaderboard becomes a recurring social event (think GitHub Trending, but kindness‑ranked).

5. **“Door‑O‑Meter” embeddable widget**  
   After a contributor merges through OpenDoor, they get a live SVG badge for their GitHub profile/website: “Doors opened: 3 | Top repo: S‑tier”. Every README with that badge is a free ad. Give them a one‑click copy for their GitHub profile.

6. **“Merge Streak” social‑first trophies**  
   Gamify consecutive weeks with a merged PR. Each streak unlocks a unique, beautiful art card (think Spotify Wrapped style) — “3‑week merge streak”, “5 repos in a month”. No crypto, just insanely shareable visuals. People post streaks before they post resumes.

7. **“DoorPal” browser extension**  
   A tiny extension that overlays C‑Rank, response time, and competition radar directly on GitHub issue/repo pages. Free, with a “Pro” login unlock for personal recommendations. The extension’s install base becomes a distribution channel; “Install DoorPal, stop guessing” is easy to share.

8. **Auto‑generated “Weekend Letter” (audio + text)**  
   Every Saturday, OpenDoor compiles the week’s top doors, worst ghostings, and funniest roast, turned into a 3‑minute AI‑narrated audio clip + text post. One‑click share to WhatsApp, Reddit, Telegram. It’s a podcast that builds itself from your own data.
