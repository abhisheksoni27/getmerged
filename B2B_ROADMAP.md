# 🏛️ GetMerged B2B Monetization Roadmap

> ⚠️ **STATUS: PARKED UNTIL 10,000 OPTED-IN DEVELOPER PROFILES**
> 
> The B2B recruitment & enterprise monetization features below require critical mass on the supply side. We will not build or market these features until GetMerged reaches **10,000+ active, verified contributor profiles** opted into opportunities on WorkDone Database.

---

## 📋 Parked B2B Features & Specifications

### 1. 🔔 The "Founder Talent Radar" (Slack & Discord Webhook)
* **Target Audience**: CTOs, Engineering Leads, Technical Founders.
* **Pricing**: $99/month.
* **Core Value**: Real-time webhook notifications pushed to internal `#engineering-hiring` Slack channels the instant an engineer with Level 4+ Karma in target repos (`kubernetes/*`, `envoyproxy/*`, `tokio-rs/*`) toggles "Open to Opportunities".
* **Trigger Condition**: 10,000 opted-in developer profiles.

---

### 2. 🎯 The "Ecosystem Poaching Radar" (Repo-to-Candidate Sourcing)
* **Target Audience**: Startups building alternatives to open-source infrastructure (e.g. Datadog, Temporal, Supabase, Redis).
* **Pricing**: $199/month.
* **Core Value**: Search by target repository URL (e.g., `temporalio/temporal`) to see the ranked leaderboard of top external, non-employee contributors who are open to full-time or advisory roles.
* **Trigger Condition**: 15,000 opted-in developer profiles.

---

### 3. 🔒 "GetMerged for Private Orgs" (Internal Code Review Bottleneck Radar)
* **Target Audience**: VP of Engineering & Tech Leads managing 20+ software engineers.
* **Pricing**: $299/month ($29/seat).
* **Core Value**: Connect private GitHub orgs to compute internal C-Rank, maintainer review latency heatmaps, and PR size sweet spots, alerting tech leads when team PRs stall sprints.
* **Trigger Condition**: 5,000 GitHub Org installs.

---

### 4. 💵 Paid 30-Min "Founder Architecture Consultations" (Reverse Bounties)
* **Target Audience**: Funded AI & cloud infrastructure startups.
* **Pricing**: $250–$500 per completed consult (GetMerged takes 15% platform fee).
* **Core Value**: Founders attach a direct architecture consultation fee to candidate outreach, guaranteeing a 100% reply rate from top 1% principal engineers without recruiter agency fees.
* **Trigger Condition**: 25,000 opted-in developer profiles.
