# Jira Story: "Aha!" Instant Onboarding Matcher Wizard

## Epic Link
EPIC-201: Homepage Revamp & User Retention

## Description
As a first-time visitor, I want a quick, delightful 3-second selection wizard so that I can see personalized repository matches immediately without scrolling through generic lists.

## Why
First-time visitors drop off within the first 10 seconds if they do not find immediate relevance. By prompting users with a simple two-click micro-wizard (Select Language -> Select Experience level), we trigger the "Aha!" moment immediately by filtering out irrelevant projects.

## Specific Key Result
- **Early Retention (D1)**: Retain 60% of first-time organic visitors by converting them to active explorers.
- **Bounce Rate**: Decrease immediate bounce rate by 30%.

## How to Solve (Solution)
1. **Hero Matcher Box**: Design a neobrutalist card inside the hero section containing the wizard.
2. **Step 1 (Language)**: Quick-select icons for top ecosystems (TypeScript, Rust, Go, Python, Java).
3. **Step 2 (Vibe)**: Toggle selector: "I want easy first-time issues" vs. "I want massive active codebases".
4. **Instant Match Load**: On completion, trigger the listings grid filter dynamically with a smooth fade-slide-up transition showing their personalized "Open Doors".
